# Reference notes — Le Faon

These notes override your own reading of the images. Take from each folder only what it's for.

## mood/
- **golden-hour-cardigan.png** (from the current Le Faon site): the brand's soul. Natural light,
  a cream cardigan over an ivory slip, deep forest shadow. Take the warmth, the stillness, the
  sand/cream against dark earth. This is where the palette's darkest tone comes from.
- **soft-portrait-how-to-order.png** (from the current Le Faon site): the everyday palette.
  Warm greige, blush skin tones, soft diffused light, a light serif heading over a washed image.
  This is the default page feeling.

## type/
- **existing-site-serif-heading.png**: the current site's heading style. Light, classical,
  high-contrast serif, set large, in mixed case with generous tracking. Keep this spirit for
  display type.

## layouts/
- **home-editorial-toteme.png**: take the restraint: full-bleed image with small serif caps
  laid over it, and the quiet two-up editorial tiles. Ignore the sale banner and "Shop now".
- **product-page-editorial-scroll.png**: take the idea that a product page continues as an
  editorial story below the fold (large detail images, a short line of copy, asymmetric
  placement), then "You may also like".
- **product-page-gallery.png**: take the structure: image gallery left, quiet details right,
  thin-ruled accordions. On mobile this becomes a swipeable gallery with dots.
- **editorial-split-grid.png**: take the asymmetric mix of small and large images and the
  wide-tracked uppercase label ("ELEGANCE & TIMELESS"). Ignore the button style.
- **lookbook-collection-grid.png**: take the catalog grid: consistent portrait crops on a
  pale background, lots of air, a collection intro with a thin rule.

## details/
- **size-and-colorway-swatches.png**: size options as small text with a thin circle on the
  selected size; colorways shown as small photo swatches (not flat color chips) that switch
  the gallery.
- **accordion-and-size-picker.png**: thin hairline accordions (Description, Materials, …),
  small square size boxes, a quiet "Size guide" link.

## Conflicts with the brief (the brief wins)
- Every reference shows prices, "Add to bag", quantity steppers, wishlist hearts or sale
  banners. Le Faon has none of these: no prices, no cart, no wishlist, no sales. The action
  is "Add to inquiry".
- Most references use pure white or cool grey backgrounds. Le Faon is warmer: bone and linen.
- Several references use heavy black buttons. Ours are deep earth (`--inverted`), never black.
